Owner Earnings = Net Income + D&A − Maintenance CapEx (Buffett 1986 method)
Base IV: historical revenue CAGR, 10% discount, 2-stage (Yrs 1-5 full / Yrs 6-10 half rate)
Bear = 60% growth, 11% discount | Bull = 130% growth, 9% discount
Maint CapEx%: High-growth >15% = 55% maint | Moderate 5-15% = 70% | Mature <5% = 85%
MOS = (Intrinsic Value − Price) / IV — positive = undervalued, >25% = BUY zone

How to read this — net conviction = investors adding or opening, minus investors trimming
Each row is one stock across all 25 tracked investors. Net = (adding + new) − trimming. Agreement is that net as a share of the investors holding it, so +50% means half of the holders moved the same way. Only names held by 2 or more investors appear — a single manager's trade isn't consensus.
